✦ A DREAM IN THE MAKING

Small beginnings.

Favéra is my vision for a future fashion company, born from my love for crochet and my desire to turn creativity into something much bigger.

HOW IT STARTED

It began with crochet.

My interest in crochet introduced me to the world of making things by hand. What started as a creative interest became something I could share with other people and earn money from.

So far, I have made over 30,000 CFA from selling my products. That experience showed me that something I enjoy creating can also become a business.

THE REALITY

A vision bigger than my current resources.

Right now, Favéra lacks the branding, social media presence, and quality photographs of my work that it needs to grow. Without these, it is difficult to showcase my products, reach new customers, and build a recognizable identity.

I know that loving what I create is only one part of building a successful company. I also need to learn how to present it, market it, and build a brand that people can recognize and trust.

WHAT COMES NEXT

From a personal passion to an international brand.

During my gap year, I plan to give Favéra the branding, visual identity, and online presence it deserves. I want to develop it beyond a personal hobby into a serious clothing business.

My long-term ambition is to grow Favéra into a million-dollar international fashion company. It is a big vision, but I want to start building towards it now, one step at a time.

This is more than something I make.
It is something I hope to build.

Favéra — a little idea with a very big future. ✦
