CHAPTER 01

JUMPSTART ACADEMY · 2023

The first spark.

Around 2023, I attended Jumpstart Academy, my first technology program. During the summer program, I learned HTML and got introduced to Arduino. I was beginning to discover that technology could be more than something I used. I could actually use it to create things.

One of the highlights was working with my group on a machine that detected muscle contraction. Our group came first, and I received the overall best leader award.

I don't have pictures from that experience anymore, but that doesn't make it any less important to my story. Jumpstart was one of the first steps in a journey I didn't yet fully understand.

Made with curiosity, creativity, and a little bit of chaos.
