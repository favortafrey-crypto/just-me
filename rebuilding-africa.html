DISCOVER THE VISION ↓

WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

It started with a birthday.

I once attended my sister's birthday celebration at an orphanage. Seeing the conditions in which the children lived stayed with me long after that day.

I wanted to do something to help. I began making plans for a foundation that would support orphans across Cameroon, because I believed that every child deserved care, dignity, and the opportunity to experience a better future.

At the time, I imagined that my contribution would mainly involve giving to those in need. Then I had an experience that expanded the way I understood change.

A DIFFERENT PERSPECTIVE

Then Rwanda changed the way I saw things.

During my visit to Rwanda this year, I was struck by the country's cleanliness, development, and the attitude toward collective responsibility that I encountered.

One idea particularly stayed with me: that a country does not belong to its government alone. It belongs to its people, too. Its improvement requires citizens who are willing to contribute to the places where they live.

That experience made me rethink my original plan. What if helping people was not only about giving to those in need, but also about bringing people together to improve the communities around them?

THE IDEA BEHIND REBUILDING AFRICA

THE VISION

From helping individuals to rebuilding communities.

Rebuilding Africa is my vision for a citizen-led initiative that brings young people together to create positive change, beginning in their own communities in Cameroon.

01

Clean Our Communities

Bring young people together to clean their neighbourhoods, care for shared spaces, and take greater responsibility for their surroundings.

02

Stand With Orphans

Support orphaned and vulnerable children through community-led acts of care, donations, and initiatives that help meet their needs.

03

Build Citizen Responsibility

Encourage young people to understand that improving a country is a shared responsibility and that meaningful change can begin with small, local actions.

WHERE WE BEGIN

Start with our community. Grow from there.

I want Rebuilding Africa to begin with young people working together in their own communities in Cameroon. Rather than starting with the expectation of changing an entire country at once, we can begin by identifying local needs, organizing practical initiatives, and encouraging others to participate.

My hope is that these efforts will grow into a wider movement of young Africans who care about their communities and are willing to contribute their time, ideas, and resources to make them better.

We cannot rebuild everything in a day.
But we can decide where to begin.
